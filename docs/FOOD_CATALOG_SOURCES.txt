Standard grocery expansion (300 entries), curated 2026-11-10

Source selection, German/English labels and row-level provenance:
tool/data/standard_food_catalog.json
Regenerate Dart data: node tool/generate_standard_food_catalog.cjs

1. Public Health England, McCance and Widdowson's Composition of Foods
   Integrated Dataset (CoFID), 2021, worksheet 1.3 Proximates.
   https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/composition-of-foods-integrated-dataset-cofid
   https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/60538b91e90e07527df82ae4/McCance_Widdowsons_Composition_of_Foods_Integrated_Dataset_2021..xlsx
   Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government
   Licence v3.0. https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/
   Food codes are retained in each entry. Protein, fat and carbohydrate are
   read per 100 g edible portion. Carbohydrate is converted from monosaccharide
   equivalents following the user guide p16: known starch / 1.10, remaining
   carbohydrate / 1.05 (general conversion where individual sugars are not
   fully available). Values are rounded to 0.01 g. 'Tr' is rounded to zero; missing
   values ('N') are never silently treated as zero.

2. USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy (April 2018).
   https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/
   https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_csv_2018-04.zip
   Used for 26 entries without complete CoFID macros. Exact FDC IDs and food
   descriptions are retained. Protein (1003) and fat (1004) are copied;
   available carbohydrate is total carbohydrate (1005) minus fibre (1079),
   rounded to 0.01 g. All four source nutrients must be present.

3. Cacao nibs: KoRo KAKAO_017, 100% roasted cocoa beans, manufacturer label.
   https://www.koro.com/bede/bio-fairtrade-kakaonibs-500-g
   Per 100 g: protein 12 g, carbohydrate 29 g, fat 53 g.
   This is a representative product value, not a universal laboratory assay.

Names distinguish dry, cooked, fresh and preserved food when needed. Prepared
foods and recipes are reference averages; specific brands and recipes vary.
Energy continues to use the app's existing 4/4/9 macro calculation and can
differ from a source's reported energy. Extra nutrients in existing app
flows remain estimates from FoodNutrientEstimator, not imported lab values.
No health-focus labels are assigned automatically to the expansion.

Existing duplicate display entries are collapsed via explicit aliases; stored
food history and recipe macros are not rewritten. 'Eier' and 'Hühnereier'
resolve to one catalog identity, displayed as 'Hühnerei' / 'Chicken egg'.
